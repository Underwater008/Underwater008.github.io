Fall 2025 · IML 575 Final
The first version.

For my IML 575 final at USC, I built a website around the JUNK Consortium — an interactive Archipelago where each member of the lab appeared as a distinct island.

The assignment didn't require a website. I made one anyway, on top of the standard deliverable.

From class project to commission
Out of courtesy,
it was free.

I delivered the standard IML 575 assignment and a working website for the lab — at no cost.

A few months later, Alex McDowell asked if I would design the website for the entire JUNK Consortium worldwide.

Email · Alex McDowell · Mar 31
The commission
One site for the whole consortium.

Each member school had its own site. Most were barely usable, with URLs nobody could remember.

Alex asked me to build a single hub — one place where every school can upload, edit, and publish their own JUNK projects.

USC Purdue UBC Miami Nebraska UEC + more
